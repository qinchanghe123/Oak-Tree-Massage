How early should I arrive?

Arriving a few minutes before your massage session can help you settle in. If you have specific timing questions, please call us in advance.

Can I get a massage if I have an injury?

If you have a recent injury, ongoing symptoms, or a health concern, consult a qualified healthcare professional about whether massage is appropriate. You can also call us before visiting to discuss your questions. Massage is not a substitute for medical treatment.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please call (737) 477-4565 to confirm the current cancellation and rescheduling policy. We do not want to guess at terms that may change.

Is there parking available?

For up-to-date parking and arrival directions at 200 E Cypress Creek Road, Suite 107, please give us a call.

Do you offer cupping?

Cupping appears on our menu as a $20 add-on. Please ask about availability and suitability when you call.
